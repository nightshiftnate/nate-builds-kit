# stats (keyword: STATS)

The prompt from the "82% skipped in 3 seconds" reel. Claude reads your reel numbers through Metricool and tells you which openings lost people fastest. Then you watch those openings yourself.

Metricool is a social media app that tracks your posts. It's made by Metricool, not me, and it isn't sponsoring this.

## 1. Connect Metricool to Claude

1. Make a Metricool account at [metricool.com](https://metricool.com) and connect your Instagram to it.
2. In Claude, open Customize, then Connectors, and add Metricool.
3. Sign in to Metricool when it asks, and allow access.

Before you ask, check that your reels show up in Metricool.

## 2. The prompt

Paste this into Claude, word for word what I asked on screen:

```text
Which of my reels lost people fastest in the first three seconds?
```

Claude lines up each reel with its skip rate and average watch time. Skip rate is the share of views that left in the first three seconds (that's how Instagram defines it).

Want more out of it? Follow up with:

```text
Show the same table for my last 20 reels, sorted from most skipped to least, with the post date next to each one.
```

## 3. Watch the worst one

This is the part Claude can't do for you. Open the reel at the top of the list and watch only its first three seconds. Ask yourself what a stranger sees before they swipe.

Then do the same for your best one and compare the two openings.

## Notes

- Your numbers won't look like mine. Small accounts swing a lot from reel to reel.
- If Claude says it can't find skip rate, ask for "Instagram reel insights" by name, or check that the reel shows up in Metricool first.
