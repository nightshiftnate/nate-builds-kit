# Switch off your shared chat links in Claude and ChatGPT

From the "Your shared Claude chats can end up on Google" carousel.

When you hit Share on a chat, you make a public link. Anyone who has that link can read the chat. In July 2026, [WIRED reported](https://www.wired.com/story/private-claude-chats-exposed-in-google-and-bing-search-results/) shared Claude chats turning up in Google and Bing searches.

Your private chats were not affected. Only chats someone made a share link for. But every link you ever made still works until you switch it off.

## Claude: shared chats

Use the web app or the desktop app.

1. Click your name in the bottom left, then **Settings**.
2. Open **Privacy**.
3. Next to **Shared chats**, click **Manage**. You see every chat you have shared, with the date.
4. Click **Unshare** on anything you don't need public. The link stops working right away.

You can also do it from inside one chat: click **Share** and set it to private.

## Claude: shared artifacts

Artifacts have their own sharing, so check them one by one.

1. Open the artifact.
2. Click **Share**.
3. Under **Who has access**, choose **Only you** (Pro, Max and Ultra) or **Only people invited** (Team and Enterprise).

## ChatGPT: shared links

1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Open **Data controls**.
3. Next to **Shared links**, click **Manage**.
4. Click **Delete shared link** on each one, or open **More actions** and pick **Delete all shared links**.

## What switching off does and doesn't do

- It kills the link. Anyone who opens it after that can't read the chat.
- It does not delete the chat from your own history. Delete the chat separately if you want it gone.
- It can't remove a copy, screenshot or archive someone already made.
- If a chat already showed up in Google, switching the link off is the first step. Google drops dead pages on its own schedule, and its Remove outdated content tool can speed that up.

Claude's help page puts it simply: treat a public link as public.
